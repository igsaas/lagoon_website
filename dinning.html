Taste the Coast

Dining with the ocean as your backdrop

At Rivora, dining is designed around the rhythms of Koh Rong’s coastline. From sunlit breakfasts on the terrace to candlelit dinners by the waves, each meal is rooted in locally sourced seafood, tropical produce, and bold culinary techniques.

Whether you arrive for a quick coffee, a celebratory banquet, or a chef-led tasting, our team curates every detail so you can fully unwind.
